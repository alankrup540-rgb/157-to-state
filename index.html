Suggested weeks

The Train tab can fill any week with these, and you can swap anything after. If soccer is still going, it replaces the Wednesday and Saturday sprints. If a dual lands the day after a lift, move the lift.

In-season rules

Team practice comes first, and your coach's schedule beats this one.

  • Lift twice a week for 30 minutes, doing only the first two lifts of A or B at 3 sets each.
  • Don't lift hard the day before a competition.
  • Do neutral work three days a week.
  • Take Sunday off.
  • Only add conditioning if practice was easy.

Neutral work

Pick your two best shots and two setups for each. Shoot off a tie or after moving their head, not from open space.

  1. Motion, 3 min: stance, level changes and circling.
  2. Setups, 5 min: setup into shot at full speed, 10 reps each side.
  3. Finishes, 4 min: start on the leg with your partner fighting back, then finish straight into a tilt or cradle.
  4. Defense, 3 min: sprawl, whizzer, head down, spin behind.

Twice a week, also do 5 x 1-minute neutral-only goes and restart after every takedown. Include one go starting down 1 with 30 seconds left.

Takedown log

After every match, add each takedown for or against you on the Log tab. "Neutral" could mean getting shot on, or getting caught on your own shots, and those need different fixes. After 5 matches, spend most of your drill time on the biggest leak the log shows.

Weight

  • Gain about half a pound a week until you're near 157, and don't cut.
  • Eat three meals and two snacks, with about 110-150 g of protein a day.
  • Sleep at least 8 hours.

Back and neck

Do the 5-minute routine after training. In your stance, bend at the hips instead of rounding your lower back. Skip heavy back squats and regular deadlifts until your back feels normal.

See a trainer or doctor if it's still sore after 2 weeks, or it hurts when you arch or bridge. Lower back stress fractures are common in teen wrestlers.

Stop wrestling until you're checked if you get numbness, tingling, weakness or pain shooting down an arm or leg.

Peak

  • From Jan 25: cut drilling by about a third, but keep it fast. Lift once a week, and go light.
  • Week of each postseason meet: no hard lifting, and keep the last 3 days short and quick.
  • Regionals: try to win yours, since placing higher usually means a better sectional draw.
